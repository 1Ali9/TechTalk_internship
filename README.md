Agile Project Management and Oversight 
My contributions to the team's agile practices emphasized collaboration, clear communication, and 
structured progress tracking. I actively supported the team's workflow by participating in key planning 
activities and utilizing the tools that kept everyone aligned. 

● Extensive Role in Planning Structure and Architecture: 
I played an extensive role in planning the structure and architecture of the website we were building by 
attending multiple meetings with the team. In these sessions, I suggested concrete ideas such as Solar 
Share and Rebuild Lebanon as practical examples to help the team better visualize the website's layout, 
user flows, and overall user experience. We thoroughly discussed the functional requirements (such as 
core user interactions and service matching features) and non-functional requirements (including 
performance, security, and scalability needs). These discussions also covered strategies for monetizing 
the website, ways to attract freelancers by highlighting benefits and ease of use, methods to encourage 
greater freelancer engagement and retention, approaches to effectively connect freelancers with 
clients, and the creation of administrator roles that would oversee the platform to maintain quality and 
compliance. My input during these meetings helped clarify the project's direction and ensured that the 
architecture would support long-term growth and usability. 

● GitHub for Version Control and Branching Strategy:  
We utilized GitHub as our primary version control system to maintain a clean and organized codebase. 
Whenever I was assigned a task, I would open a new branch to work in isolation, preventing any 
disruptions to the main codebase. Upon completion, I would submit the changes for review by the team, 
who would examine the code to ensure it integrated seamlessly into the main branch without conflicts. 
To enhance parallel development, we implemented sub-branches and decided to split the team into two 
focused groups: one dedicated to the client side of the application and the other handling the 
administrator and specialists sections. This structured branching approach improved coordination, 
reduced merge issues, and allowed different parts of the project to progress efficiently at the same 
time.

ensure that deliverables remained on track. By keeping detailed notes on task completion and any

Centralized Resources with Notion 

● Notion for Resource Centralization and Consistency: We relied on Notion to centralize all project 
resources, environmental variables, and supporting articles. The centralized approach minimized 
confusion, reduced onboarding time for any new details, and ensured consistency of information across 
all team members, regardless of which part of the application they were working on.
